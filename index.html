11 · The Logic of Running

The ground moves, not the runner

The runner stays in one spot. The track slides toward him.

Trains, coins and barriers look small when they’re far away, and get bigger as they come closer.

That’s what makes it feel fast.

track_y += speed size = depth
12 · The Logic of Moving

How the keys work

Left and right change which lane he’s in.

Jump pushes him up, and gravity pulls him back down.

Slide makes him shorter for a second.

if key == LEFT: lane -= 1 vy = -12 # jump vy += 0.8 # gravity
13 · The Logic of Coins

Touch a coin, get a point

Coins show up in a line in one lane.

They move toward the runner, like everything else.

When the runner touches a coin, the score goes up by 1 and the coin is gone.

Coins can also be spent on power-ups, like a shield or a coin magnet.

if touching(player, coin): score += 1 coins.remove(coin)
14 · The Logic of Obstacles

Trains and barriers

We keep a list of obstacles.

New ones start far away, and we delete them once they pass the runner.

Every obstacle has an invisible box around it. If the runner’s box touches it, the police catch up. Hit two obstacles and it’s game over.

if touching(player, obstacle): hits += 1 police_closer() if hits == 2: game_over()
15 · The Police Behind You

The police keep chasing

The police run right behind the runner.

Sometimes they fall back, and the runner is alone for a few seconds.

Then, at a random moment, they suddenly show up again. It keeps the player nervous.

if random() < 0.01: police_away = not police_away
